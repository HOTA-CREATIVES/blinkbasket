# User Manuals - BlinkBasket

Welcome to the BlinkBasket user manuals directory! Refer to the guides below corresponding to your role.

---

## 1. Customer User Guide

### 1.1 Setting Up Your Delivery Address
*   On your first login, select your village region from the manual dropdown list.
*   Provide local landmarks, house numbers, or specific directions to help our riders locate you quickly.

### 1.2 Shopping and Checkout
*   Browse categories on the home screen or search for items.
*   Add items by tapping the **ADD** button on product cards.
*   Click the green Cart bar at the bottom to verify receipt details.
*   Click **PLACE ORDER**. All orders are strictly **Cash on Delivery (COD)**; pay the rider in cash or UPI scan when they arrive.

---

## 2. Delivery Partner Guide

### 2.1 Managing Duty Status
*   Toggle your online switch in the top app bar to start receiving orders.

### 2.2 Order Transitions
*   When assigned an order, verify the customer address and total Cash to collect.
*   Tapping **PICK UP ITEMS** signals you are loading bags at the store.
*   Tapping **START DELIVERY** notifies the customer you are in transit.
*   Upon arrival, collect the total cash, check the confirmation dialog box, and click **MARK DELIVERED**.
